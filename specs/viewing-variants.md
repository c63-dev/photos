# Viewing Variants Specification

## Purpose
Defines the medium-size viewing image for photos and the lower-resolution playback rendition for large videos, generated in the background so that viewing on a phone or over a home connection is fast.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Viewing Image
The system SHALL generate, for every image, a viewing variant whose longest side is a configurable size, defaulting to 2048 pixels, in the same output format and quality rules as thumbnails. Images already at or below that size SHALL NOT get a variant; the original is used.

#### Scenario: Large photo
- **WHEN** a 6000x4000 photo is indexed
- **THEN** a 2048x1365 viewing variant is generated

#### Scenario: Small photo
- **WHEN** a 1600x1200 photo is indexed
- **THEN** no viewing variant is generated

### Requirement: Playback Rendition
The system SHALL generate, for every video whose resolution exceeds a configurable threshold (default 1080p), a playback rendition at that threshold in the default video format with the audio preserved. Videos at or below the threshold SHALL NOT get a rendition.

#### Scenario: 4K video
- **WHEN** a 3840x2160 video is indexed
- **THEN** a 1920x1080 playback rendition is generated

### Requirement: Serving Variants
A `file` request MAY ask for the `view` or `play` variant. If the variant exists it SHALL be served with the same caching rules as thumbnails; if it does not exist yet, the original SHALL be served instead. Listings SHALL indicate which variants exist for each item.

#### Scenario: Variant not ready
- **WHEN** the playback rendition of a video is requested before it has been generated
- **THEN** the original video is served

### Requirement: Generated By The Worker
Variants SHALL be work items in the background queue, generated after thumbnails for the same item, and SHALL follow the same browsing signals, caching, failure and resource rules as thumbnails.

#### Scenario: Browsed folder
- **WHEN** a visitor lists a folder with no variants yet
- **THEN** that folder's variants move to the front of the queue behind its thumbnails
